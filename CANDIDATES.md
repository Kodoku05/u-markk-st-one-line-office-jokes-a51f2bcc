# One-Line Office Jokes: drafts and selection

Scores are editorial estimates, not judge results. Each criterion is scored 1–5;
the criteria remain in the judge's priority order. Totals help compare drafts,
but do not override the priority order. Freshness means fresh wording as drafted
here; it is not a claim that every joke on the internet has been checked.

1. **Office joke:** a single-line joke about office life.
2. **Real detail:** a recognizable office detail drives the joke.
3. **Final beat:** the laugh arrives in the last few words.
4. **Clarity:** understandable on one reading.
5. **Freshness:** avoids familiar phrasing and stock punchlines.

## The five supplied drafts

1. My boss told me to take ownership of the coffee machine, so I did — it's mine now, and I charge the office rent to store it.
2. My calendar auto-declined a meeting titled 'Quick sync' because even the software knows nothing quick has ever happened in that room.
3. The boss asked me to 'circle back' so many times I billed it on my timesheet as cardio, and finance approved it as a wellness expense.
4. I put my out-of-office on for a sick day, and my boss replied: 'Thanks — I've forwarded your fever to the team for visibility.'
5. I asked IT why the Wi-Fi dies at 4 p.m. every Friday, and they said the router clocks out before we do — it's the only one here with boundaries.

## Three new drafts

6. HR replaced the suggestion box with a QR code that opens our competitor's careers page.
7. Our boss banned headphones to improve communication; now we all know he's interviewing elsewhere.
8. The CEO cut costs by making the suggestion box a swear jar; it now funds his bonus.

## Selection and one further refinement

Draft 8 narrowly beats draft 4: the cause and consequence read more cleanly, and
the high-status beneficiary is also the target. It uses a perverse policy with a
financial consequence. Its limitation is that swear jars are a familiar comic
device; the suggestion-box-to-executive-bonus mechanism supplies the new turn.

Refinement: cut the explanatory "cut costs by making" in favor of "replaced";
make the suggestion box belong to the staff with "our"; replace the general
"it now funds" with a completed consequence and a short office-time kicker.

**Final:** The CEO replaced our suggestion box with a swear jar; his bonus was funded by lunch.
